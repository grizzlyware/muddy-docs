# Muddy Booking Colour Scheme Settings

## Location
- URL: `/settings/branding`
- Navigation: Settings > Branding > Colour palette section

## Available Colour Palette Options
Shown as small tiles. Custom appears first, then the eight preset schemes:

1. **Custom** (first tile): build your own palette. Shows "+ Your colours" until a custom palette exists
2. Muddy
3. Frosty Paws
4. Pawsh Pink
5. Red Set Go!
6. Pumpkin Pup
7. Bark Mode
8. Noble Paws
9. Splashy Paws

Presets are shown in this order. Main colours: Muddy dark green #1A3629 / mint #68EDB3; Frosty Paws navy #20234B / icy blue #92E4FF; Pawsh Pink plum #54215D / pink #FB87D2; Red Set Go! deep red #6F191A / coral #FF8082; Pumpkin Pup orange #C34200 / amber #FBAA31; Bark Mode charcoal #202020 / mint #68EDB3; Noble Paws charcoal #32373C / gold #B89F5C / teal #44A1A0; Splashy Paws teal #007573 / pink #CC3E8E.

Total: 1 Custom + 8 preset schemes = 9 tiles

## Custom Palette
When Custom is selected, two additional controls appear:
- **Brand colour** and **Accent colour** rows: each shows swatches (logo colours, plus colours picked this session) and a rainbow + button that opens the browser's own colour picker ("Choose any colour")
- **Get colours from your logo**: If a logo is uploaded, Muddy suggests swatches extracted from it. If no logo, shows "Upload your logo above and we'll pick out its colours for you."
- A new custom palette starts from the logo's two main colours (brand from the first, accent from the second). With no logo it starts from Muddy dark green #1A3629
- Help text: "This colour palette applies to your booking forms and admin area. When you create a custom palette, we may adjust your colours to maintain contrast and readability."

## Live Preview
- A sample booking page (the operator's own business name, "Book now", date selector, time slots, "Continue") is shown under the palette tiles. It only shows once a palette is selected.
- Preview updates live as you change palette or colours.
- Caption: "How your booking pages will look. Try it out."
- This replaced the old "no preview" behaviour.

## How it Works
- Colour palette applies to both booking forms AND admin area
- Click a scheme tile to select it
- For Custom: pick brand colour and accent colour
- Click Save to apply changes
- Changes take effect immediately after saving
