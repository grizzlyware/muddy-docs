## What are opening times?

Opening times are the hours customers can book. You set them separately for each offering, so different services or locations can have different hours.

For one-off closures such as holidays, see [How to add a block out](adding-block-outs.md).

If you haven't set up your offerings yet, start with [Setting up your offerings](creating-a-walk.md).

## Setting opening times for an offering

1. Click **Offerings** in the left-hand menu
2. Click the offering you want to set hours for
3. Click **Settings**
4. Click **Opening times**

![Offering settings dropdown with Opening times highlighted](../screenshots/offering-settings-dropdown-opening-times-1792672037303.png)

You'll see a row for each day of the week. Set each day to the hours that offering is available.

### Using preset times for all days

To set every day at once:

1. Choose a preset from the **Apply preset to all days** menu near the top of the page, such as **24/7**, **09:00 to 17:00**, **08:00 to 20:00** or **Closed**
2. Click **Apply**

![Offering opening times page with Apply preset menu highlighted](../screenshots/offering-opening-times-annotated-1792671951851.png)

### Setting individual days

For each day, choose one of:

- **24/7**: open all day
- A set range, such as **05:00 to 22:00**, **06:00 to 20:00** or **09:00 to 17:00**
- **Closed**: no bookings that day
- **Custom**: enter your own start and end times

Click **Save** at the bottom of the page.

### Choosing when bookings start

**Start booking slots at**, at the top of the page, sets the times a booking can start. Choose from:

- **Top of the hour**: 9:00am, 10:00am, 11:00am and so on
- **Quarter past the hour**: 9:15am, 10:15am, 11:15am and so on
- **Half past the hour**: 9:30am, 10:30am, 11:30am and so on
- **Quarter to the hour**: 8:45am, 9:45am, 10:45am and so on
- **Every fifteen minutes**: 9:00am, 9:15am, 9:30am, 9:45am and so on
- **Every thirty minutes**: 9:00am, 9:30am, 10:00am and so on

This is the default for every day. Each open day also has its own **Start booking slots at** menu, so one day can start differently from the rest. On a **Custom** day, each time range has one, so mornings can start every fifteen minutes and afternoons on the hour, for example. Leave it on **Default** to use the setting at the top of the page. Applying a preset to all days puts every day back on **Default**.

A booking starts in the time range that its start time falls in, and it can run on into a range that follows straight after. It can't run through a time when you're closed.

### Anchoring to sunrise or sunset

If your hours follow daylight, for example you always open at sunrise or close before dusk, you can anchor times to sunrise, sunset, first light or last light instead of fixed clock times. This requires a geographic location to be set on the resource. Add the location in the resource's settings, and the opening times page will offer **Sunrise to sunset** and **First light to last light** presets. With **Custom**, you can also choose sunrise, sunset, first light or last light for **Opens at** and **Closes at**, with an **Offset** to open or close a little earlier or later.

## Setting times for multiple offerings

Each offering has its own opening times, so repeat the steps above for each one. This lets you, for example, run one service only on weekdays and another at weekends.

## Tips

- **Set opening times before you go live**: an offering without opening times cannot be booked by customers.
- **Use Closed** for days an offering is never available, even if your business is open on those days.
- **Changes apply straight away** to new bookings.
